Delete account & data

Memory games · Back to the app · Privacy policy

You can delete your game data directly in the app. This page is provided for Google Play and for parents who need written instructions.

1. In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Settings (gear icon).
  2. Tap Delete my data.
  3. Confirm in the dialog.

This immediately removes on this device:

  • All player profiles and nicknames
  • Scores, records, and adventure progress
  • Cloud backup tied to your current session

If you were signed in with Google, the app also deletes your cloud rows and signs you out.

2. Need help?

If automatic deletion fails (for example offline cloud delete), contact us with the Google email used to sign in:

  • Email: moranalgazi@gmail.com
  • GitHub: open an issue

AdMob may retain advertising-related data under Google’s policies. See our privacy policy for details.
