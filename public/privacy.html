Privacy policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Back to Memory games

This privacy policy applies to Memory games, an educational application for children to practice English, math, and fractions through memory-style matching games. It covers:

  • The website at www.playmemorygames.win
  • The Android app (when installed from Google Play or built from the same project), which runs the same game inside a secure app shell

The app is intended for supervised use by children. Parents, guardians, and teachers should choose player names and decide whether to enable sign-in or cloud sync.

Information we collect

  • Player names: names entered on a device to separate scores between players. These are nicknames — we do not require real names.
  • Age range (optional bucket): when you add a player, you may choose an age range (for example 5–6, 7–8, 9–10, 10–11, or 12+). We do not ask for exact birthdates. This is stored on your device and, when cloud sync is enabled, in our database.
  • Game statistics: best times, games played, quiz scores, and similar progress, stored on your device.
  • Adventure progress summary: current adventure level and how many levels completed (not full sticker album details), for optional cloud backup and aggregated statistics.
  • Preferences: language, theme, and similar settings in local storage.
  • Device identifier: a random id generated on your device so cloud sync can tell devices apart. It is not used to track you across unrelated apps or websites.
  • Account identifier (optional): if you choose Google sign-in or cloud sync, an anonymous or Google-linked user id from our auth provider (Supabase) associates saved progress with your account. If you sign in with Google, we may also receive your email address and basic profile name or photo from Google — only what Google shares for sign-in. We do not read your Gmail, contacts, or other Google data.
  • Online play: multiplayer connects two browsers or app instances directly (peer-to-peer). We do not store individual game moves in our database. Players share room codes with each other. Connecting may use STUN/TURN relay services (see Third-party services); those providers may process connection metadata such as IP addresses to help the session work.
  • Advertising identifiers (Android app only): when ads are shown, Google AdMob may process device and app interaction data (for example advertising id, IP address, and ad engagement) as described in Google’s Privacy Policy. We request child-directed, non-personalized treatment where applicable.
  • Consent choices (Android app): users in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and certain US states may see a Google consent message before personalized or regulated ad processing. Your choices are stored on the device by Google’s User Messaging Platform.

What we do not collect

  • Exact birthdates or government IDs
  • Location GPS (we do not request location permission)
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera (the game itself does not need them)
  • A public profile or social graph

How we use information

Data is used only to run the app:

  • Show records and adventure progress
  • Optionally sync progress across devices when you sign in
  • Enable online play between two invited players
  • Produce aggregated statistics for the maintainer (for example new players per day, age-range counts, adventure level distribution) through a password-protected admin tool — never as a public list of player names

We do not sell personal information. The website does not show advertising. We do not use your data for behavioral advertising on the website. The Android app may show ads as described below.

Android app — advertising and purchases

Website: the site at playmemorygames.win does not show ads.

Android app: the Google Play / native Android version may show ads through Google AdMob, configured for child-directed / family-appropriate treatment. Ads are non-personalized where required by law or platform policy.

  • Interstitial ads may appear after some solo game wins (natural breaks — not during gameplay or online matches).
  • Rewarded video ads are optional: the player chooses to watch a short video to earn a bonus sticker in the current album.
  • AdMob may collect device and interaction data. See Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses data in apps that use AdMob.
  • Users in regulated regions may see Google consent messages (GDPR / US state privacy) on first launch or when required.

In-app purchases: not offered at this time. If we add Google Play Billing later, we will update this policy and describe what purchase data Google shares with us (for example whether premium content is unlocked).

Where data is stored

  • On your device — browser or app local storage.
  • In the cloud (optional) — Supabase (hosted database) when cloud sync or Google sign-in is enabled.

Third-party services

Depending on how you use the app, these providers may process limited data:

  • Supabase — optional authentication and cloud storage of player stats.
  • Google Sign-In — optional account linking (only if you choose it).
  • Cloudflare / STUN-TURN — optional relay for online multiplayer when direct peer connection fails.
  • Google Fonts — web fonts on the website (font files may be loaded from Google).
  • GitHub Pages / hosting — serves the website; standard server logs may include IP address and browser type.
  • Google Play — distribution, updates, and Families policy compliance for the Android app.
  • Google AdMob — advertising in the Android app (not on the website).

Each provider has its own privacy policy and terms.

Children and parental choices

The app is designed for children learning with adult supervision. We do not knowingly collect more information than needed to run the game. Parents and guardians should:

  • Choose appropriate nicknames for players
  • Decide whether to enable Google sign-in or cloud sync
  • Supervise online play and room-code sharing
  • Review this policy before enabling Google sign-in, cloud sync, or the Android app

Your choices

  • Play without signing in — local players only, no Google account required.
  • Clear browser or app site data to remove local player names and scores.
  • Sign out from Google in the player dialog to end a linked session.
  • Request deletion of cloud data tied to your account via Delete account & data or by contacting us (see Contact) with the email used for Google sign-in, if applicable.

Data retention and security

Local data remains until you clear device storage. Cloud data is retained while your account or anonymous session exists and may be removed when you delete players or sign out, subject to our database provider’s policies. Cloud access is protected by row-level security so each user can only access their own records. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

International users

The app may be used from different countries (including Israel, the European Union, and the United States). Data may be processed in countries where our hosting and service providers operate. If you have questions about your rights under local privacy laws, contact us (see Contact). This policy is not legal advice.
